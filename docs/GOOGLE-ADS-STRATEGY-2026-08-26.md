## Executive decision

For the first paid-search launch managed from Wix, the recommended architecture is **one focused Google Ads Performance Max Leads campaign**, not multiple campaigns at once.

Current Wix documentation confirms that **new lead campaigns in Google Ads with Wix are Performance Max Leads**; new Smart campaigns can no longer be created. PMAX Leads is therefore the correct native Wix campaign type for this service business.

The first campaign should concentrate spend, learning and conversion signals on the highest-intent offer already supported by the site:

> **Automotive locksmith / emergency vehicle access and car key services**

Primary landing page:

`https://www.cerrajeros-cerca24-7.com/automotriz`

The campaign is designed to optimize for **commercial leads**, with calls as the most valuable action, followed by bookings and qualified form submissions.

## 1. Compliance gate — mandatory before ads can serve normally

Google currently requires **Advanced Verification** for locksmith services advertising in the United States and Canada.

Official Google policy:

- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7167922?hl=en
- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7167635?hl=en

Google states that locksmith advertisers in the United States are within the Advanced Verification vertical. The verification process may take several weeks. We can build the campaign and prepare all assets/configuration, but we must not treat the campaign as unrestricted until Google clears the advertiser.

Do not attempt to bypass verification by changing the business category, destination, service wording or identity.

## 4. Geographic strategy

Do **not** target all of New York State unless the operation truly services the entire state.

Locksmith demand is highly local and emergency-driven. The campaign should target only areas that technicians can serve profitably and quickly.

Recommended Wix setup:

1. Use **specific cities, ZIP codes or a radius around the actual operating area**.
2. Prioritize the real NYC service footprint rather than a state-wide location.
3. Use the smallest profitable service area first and expand only after conversion data proves the expansion is profitable.
4. Exclude or remove geographies that generate clicks/leads outside operational coverage.

Google recommends using **Presence: people in or regularly in the targeted locations** when advertisers need tight geographic control, especially for sensitive/local verticals.

Official Google source:

- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en

Final geo values must be resolved during campaign setup from the actual operating service area; they should not be invented from the website's broad use of “New York”.

## 11. Budget strategy

### Rule

The **actual launch budget should be selected from Wix/Google's live budget recommendation during campaign setup**, not guessed in advance.

However, for planning purposes, a very small budget is unlikely to generate enough data in a competitive New York local-services market.

A 2025 LocaliQ dataset covering more than 3,200 US home-services search campaigns reported a median home-services CPC around **$7.85**, conversion rate around **7.33%**, and cost per lead around **$90.92**. This is not a locksmith/NYC forecast, but it demonstrates why a $5/day campaign would provide too little useful learning data.

Benchmark source:

- https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-search-advertising-benchmarks/

### Provisional planning envelopes

These are **planning envelopes only** and are replaced by the live Wix/Google recommendation before launch:

- Conservative test: **$40/day**
- Preferred initial learning envelope: **$60–75/day**
- Higher-volume test: **$90/day+**

At $60/day, a 14-day first learning window represents about **$840** in media budget. This is a controlled testing budget, not a promise of lead volume or CPL.

If the business economics do not support this spend, launch at the lower acceptable Google/Wix recommendation and extend the learning window rather than forcing a high budget.

## 12. Learning-period rules

Wix currently advises that a Google Ads campaign can take around **14 days** to optimize and recommends avoiding campaign changes during that period unless something is clearly wrong.

Source:

- https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-tips-for-improving-your-campaign-performance

### Day 0

Launch only after:

- Advanced Verification status allows ads to serve;
- exact geo is confirmed;
- call phone is correct;
- conversion goals are correct;
- images are real and approved;
- budget is explicitly approved;
- campaign destination is `/automotriz`.

### Days 1–6

Do not react to daily noise. Check only for:

- policy/disapproval;
- calls from clearly wrong regions;
- broken URL/CTA;
- obviously irrelevant search traffic;
- conversion tracking failure.

### Day 7

First diagnostic review only. Document:

- spend;
- clicks;
- calls;
- bookings/forms;
- valid vs invalid leads;
- search-term relevance;
- asset performance.

### Day 14

First optimization window:

- remove clearly irrelevant search terms;
- adjust weak assets;
- evaluate geo leakage;
- compare valid CPL against business economics;
- adjust budget carefully if signal quality is strong.

### Day 28

Decide whether to:

- scale the automotive campaign;
- create a second Spanish campaign for residential/commercial services;
- create an English landing page and separate English campaign;
- test Google Local Services Ads as a complementary channel after verification.
